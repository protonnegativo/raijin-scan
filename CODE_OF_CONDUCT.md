# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). The full text is at that link and applies in full to this project. Here is a short summary.

## Our pledge

We want participation in raijin-scan to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, whatever their age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and patient. Give and accept constructive feedback.
- Assume good faith, and say what you need clearly.
- Focus on what is best for the project and the community.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, personal attacks, trolling or discriminatory language.
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwanted attention.
- Publishing other people's private information without permission.
- Other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and when someone represents the project in public.

## Reporting and enforcement

To report a problem, use [GitHub private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/protonnegativo/raijin-scan/security/advisories/new) and start the title with "Conduct", or, if you cannot, open an issue asking for a private contact without giving details. Reports are handled privately by the maintainer.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, lock threads, and temporarily or permanently ban people for behavior that breaks this code, following the enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant 2.1 (correction, warning, temporary ban, permanent ban).
